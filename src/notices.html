Departmental Notices — Spring 2025

Course Withdrawal Deadline — Spring 2025 Urgent

Posted by: Registrar Office • April 26, 2025

Students wishing to withdraw from any registered course for Spring 2025 must submit their Course Withdrawal Form (CW-03) to the Registrar Office (Room 101, Admin Block) by May 2, 2025. Withdrawals after this date will not be entertained and a grade of “F” will be recorded.

Workshop: Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking Open

Posted by: CS Department • April 24, 2025

A two-day workshop on Cybersecurity Fundamentals and Ethical Hacking will be held on May 3–4, 2025 in Lab 4, Block B. Participants must register via the student portal. Seats are limited to 40. A completion certificate will be issued to all attendees.

Thesis Defense Schedule — MSCS Batch 2023 Info

Posted by: Graduate Studies Office • April 21, 2025

The thesis defense sessions for MSCS Batch 2023 are scheduled for May 15–20, 2025. Students must submit two bound copies of their thesis to the department office at least seven days prior to their defense date. Individual schedules have been emailed to supervisors.

No-Objection Certificate (NOC) for Internship Applications Info

Posted by: Placement Office • April 18, 2025

Students applying for summer internships requiring a university NOC must submit their application to the Placement Office (Room 110, Block A) along with the internship offer letter. Processing time is 4–5 working days. Rush requests will not be accommodated.

Change of Classroom — CSC402 (Software Engineering) Update

Posted by: Time Table Committee • April 17, 2025

Effective April 21, 2025, the classroom for CSC402 (Software Engineering) Section B has been changed from Room 301 to Room 408, Block C. The class timings remain unchanged: Monday and Wednesday, 11:00 AM – 12:30 PM.
